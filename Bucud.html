About Blood Match

Many people who need a blood transfusion struggle to find donors with their exact blood type or other special conditions. Blood Match brings donors and recipients onto one platform so they can find each other directly, instead of relying only on partnered clinics.

Our Mission

We want to make it simple for a voluntary donor to find someone who genuinely needs their blood type, and for a recipient to build a long-term, trusted network of donors rather than searching from scratch every time.

What We Offer

Profile Setup

Add your blood type and relevant medical background so matches are accurate.
